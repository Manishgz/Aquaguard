      🤖 Bhim
      AquaGuard AI water assistant

      Hackathon pitch guide

      USP

      Sensors, behaviour change and community reporting in one low-cost loop, not just hardware.

      Scalability

      Household → apartment → ward → city. Works offline-first with LoRa/SMS fallback for rural areas.

      Tech stack

      HTML, CSS, Tailwind, vanilla JS, ultrasonic and flow sensors (ESP32), MQTT, AI anomaly detection for leaks.

      Impact

      Cuts leak losses, builds habits through the quiz and pledge, and gives local bodies live data to act on.

      Demo order (3 min): hero stats → quiz → trigger a leak on the IoT panel → file a community report → challenges → pledge.
